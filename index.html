Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 | Last Updated: November 2026 | Entity: OFF Mela Technologies (Kerala, India)

1. Commitment to User Privacy

At OFF Mela, safeguarding your personal data and maintaining transparency regarding our data processing practices is fundamental to our service. This Privacy Policy details the categories of information we collect, the lawful basis and purpose of processing, and how you can exercise your statutory rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and Google Play Store Developer Policies.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Identification Information

  • Authentication Data: Name, verified email address, Google profile picture, and mobile phone number collected via Google Sign-In or phone authentication.
  • Role Designation: Customer, Merchant, or Administrative account permission flag.
  • Feedback Submissions: Comments, ratings, bug reports, and customer satisfaction messages submitted through the in-app Feedback module.

2.2 Merchant Store Data

  • Store Details: Legal business name, trade name, commercial category (e.g. Food, Fashion, Supermarket), contact phone number, address, locality, and storefront logo/banner images.
  • Promotional Listings: Offer headlines, description, discount percentages, original and discounted prices, and promotional validity windows.

2.3 Geolocation Data (Hyperlocal Deal Calculation)

OFF Mela requires device location solely to provide nearby discount discovery within Kerala:

  • Precise Coordinates: Latitude and longitude obtained with explicit user permission while the app is actively used in the foreground.
  • Geohash Computation: Coordinates are mapped to geohash buckets to efficiently query active deals within a specific commercial radius (e.g., 5 km - 25 km).
  • No Continuous Background Tracking: Location is not tracked covertly in the background when the app is closed.

2.4 Technical Identifiers & Notifications

  • Push Notification Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens required to deliver saved deal alerts, offer review status, and essential administrative updates.
  • Device Metadata: OS version, device model, and app version for crash reporting and security.

3. Purpose of Processing & Use of Data

Your data is processed strictly for the following operational needs:

  1. Delivering Hyperlocal Offers: Computing distances to nearby stores and filtering valid promotional discounts.
  2. Merchant Listing Management: Enabling retailers to publish, edit, and boost deals.
  3. Push Notifications: Transmitting notifications for favorited stores, expiring coupons, and status changes.
  4. Fraud Mitigation & Platform Integrity: Preventing bot scraping, coupon churning, and fraudulent multi-accounting.
Data Sale Prohibition: OFF Mela does not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal or location data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

4. Cloud Infrastructure & Third-Party Processors

We partner with enterprise-grade cloud providers upholding stringent security protocols:

  • Google Cloud Platform & Firebase: Authentication, Firestore NoSQL database, Cloud Storage (for images), and Google Cloud Functions (isolated server endpoints).
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Push notification delivery.

All data in transit is encrypted using TLS/HTTPS 1.3, and data at rest is protected with AES-256 encryption.

5. Your Rights: Complete Account & Data Deletion

In adherence to the Indian DPDP Act and Google Play Store policies, every user possesses the absolute right to complete data erasure:

  • Self-Service In-App Deletion: You can permanently delete your account directly through Profile > Delete Account (Customers) or Merchant Profile > Delete Merchant Account (Merchants).
  • Automated Execution: Deletion is processed instantaneously by our secure microservice (`Offer Server`), wiping authentication credentials, profile data, saved offers, notifications, and merchant store records.
  • Support Email Request: Users unable to access the mobile application may also request account erasure by sending an email from their registered address to privacy@offmela.com.

For complete architectural details, visit the Account Deletion Specification & Workflow.

6. Data Retention & 30-Day Cooling-Off Period

To balance user privacy with fraud prevention and promo protection:

  • Immediate Erasure: Personal profile data and auth accounts are purged immediately upon confirmed request.
  • 30-Day Anti-Abuse Cooldown: A cryptographic, non-reversible hash of the user’s primary identifier (phone or email) is retained in an audit log for 30 days to prevent repeat signup abuse (e.g. creating accounts to consume one-time promotional welcome bonuses and immediately deleting). Re-registration within 30 days is treated as a returning subscriber.
  • Tax & Statutory Records: Merchant transaction summaries and invoice references are archived for periods required under Indian tax and commercial regulations.

7. Children's Privacy

OFF Mela is not intended for individuals under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If identified, such accounts and data are purged immediately.
